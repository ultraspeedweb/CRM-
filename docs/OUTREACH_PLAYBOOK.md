## First message — Arabic

مرحبًا {{name}}، لاحظت أن فريقكم يخدم مستثمرين أجانب بعدة لغات. نطوّر SatışDesk للفرق العقارية: يحوّل عميل واتساب إلى مسؤول واضح، متابعة، موعد وصفقة داخل مسار واحد. جهّزت عرضًا قصيرًا يناسب عملكم: رسالة عربية/تركية ← تأهيل ← متابعة ← معاينة ← صفقة. هل يناسبكم اتصال 20 دقيقة هذا الأسبوع؟

## First message — English

Hi {{name}}, I noticed your team serves international buyers in several languages. We are building SatışDesk for real-estate sales teams: one workflow from a WhatsApp enquiry to owner, follow-up, viewing and deal. I prepared a 12-minute demo around an Arabic/Turkish buyer journey. Would a 20-minute call this week be useful?

## Follow-up sequence

- Day 0: personalized first message with one specific observation from the company's public website.
- Day 2: send a 45-second screen recording of that company's example workflow; no generic product tour.
- Day 5: ask one diagnostic question: “How does the manager know which WhatsApp enquiries have no next action today?”
- Day 9: send the Founding Pilot scope and the exact TRY 50,000 price.
- Day 14: close the loop politely and move the opportunity to nurture.

## Discovery questions

1. How many new enquiries arrive in a normal week, and from which channels?
2. How many languages and salespeople share those enquiries?
3. Where is lead ownership recorded today?
4. How does a manager find an enquiry that has not received a reply or next action?
5. What happens between first WhatsApp message and property viewing?
6. Which CRM or spreadsheet is used, and why does the team bypass it?
7. What is one additional closed transaction worth to the company?
8. Who owns Meta Business/WhatsApp access and can approve the pilot?

## Proposal rule

Do not discount the TRY 50,000 pilot before removing scope. If price is the objection, offer a narrower 30-day pilot for one team/one channel, not unlimited custom development. Collect payment before kickoff; Meta usage fees remain separate.

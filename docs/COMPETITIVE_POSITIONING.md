# SatışDesk Competitive Positioning — August 2026

## Where the market is

- [respond.io](https://respond.io/pricing) starts at USD 79/month and sells a mature omnichannel inbox; its Growth tier at USD 159/month adds workflows, AI agents, broadcasts, advanced reporting and integrations. WhatsApp usage fees are separate.
- [Follow Up Boss](https://www.followupboss.com/pricing?lang=en) starts at USD 69/user/month and is deep in real estate: lead-source connections, distribution, action plans, calling/text/email, calendar, reporting and mobile. Its Pro package is USD 499/month for 10 users with onboarding and team inboxes.
- [SleekFlow](https://sleekflow.io/faq) states a USD 79/month starting price and positions advanced automation and AI in higher plans; WhatsApp fees are separate.

## Our position

SatışDesk should not market itself as “another CRM with more features.” Mature competitors win on integration count, mobile apps, automation depth and years of support operations. Our wedge is narrower:

> A managed multilingual WhatsApp-to-sale operating system for Turkish real-estate teams serving Arabic, Turkish and English buyers.

The first customer buys a working sales process and assisted rollout, not only software access. That justifies the TRY 50,000 Founding Pilot while the recurring self-service product is still maturing.

## What is already credible

- Organization-level isolation and role-based RLS.
- Auth, onboarding, leads, follow-ups, appointments and deals.
- Arabic-first interface with Turkish-market pricing and Istanbul time handling.
- Signed WhatsApp webhook receiver, idempotent external IDs, inbox storage and outbound reply path awaiting Meta credentials.
- Reproducible quality gate, pinned dependencies, generated database types, tests and a production build.

## Gaps before claiming parity

| P1 | Operational reporting and response-time SLA | Required for manager value and renewal proof |
| P1 | Real automation executor | Do not market placeholder rule storage as working automation |
| P2 | Full Turkish and English UI | Add after the first Arabic-first pilot proves the workflow |
| P2 | Mobile app, calling, portal integrations and broad omnichannel | Integrate selectively from paid demand; do not chase competitor breadth now |
| P2 | Billing, self-service subscriptions, SSO and 2FA | Needed for scalable SaaS/enterprise, not for four assisted pilots |

## Win strategy

1. Demo the prospect's exact WhatsApp journey with its languages and team roles.
2. Sell deployment speed and managed adoption; competitors mainly sell platform access.
3. Tie the pilot to manager outcomes: every lead has an owner, next action, appointment and pipeline value.
4. Use one real customer case study before expanding channels or verticals.
5. After four paid pilots, decide whether the durable moat is localization, real-estate workflow templates, or managed implementation—based on retention and usage, not opinion.

# SatışDesk — Product Master Plan

Status: **LOCKED PRODUCT NORTH STAR / Source of Truth**
Updated: 2026-10-04

## 0. Locked Product Thesis

SatışDesk is **NOT a traditional CRM, generic dashboard product, or AI chatbot attached to CRUD screens**.

SatışDesk is a **Governed Autonomous Revenue & Growth Operating System** for companies.

The CRM is only an internal data layer. The commercial product is the operating system that understands the company's goals, customers, team, products, inventory, channels, offers, orders, revenue, margin and operational events; detects gaps and opportunities; recommends the next best decisions; executes approved actions through tools and automations; measures the outcome; and learns from the result.

North-star loop:

**Sense -> Understand -> Predict -> Decide -> Approve/Policy -> Act -> Verify -> Learn**

Business loop:

**Goal -> Diagnose -> Plan -> Execute -> Measure -> Learn -> Re-plan**

A screen that only displays information is incomplete. Important information should progress toward:

**Signal -> Insight -> Decision -> Action -> Verified Result**

SatışDesk must compete through measurable business outcomes, operational intelligence, multilingual/omnichannel execution, security and governance — not by copying conventional CRM menus.

## 1. Core Promise

- The employee knows the highest-value action to perform now and why.
- The company owner knows whether the business will reach its goals, why it is ahead/behind, and what actions can close the gap.
- The system identifies winning products, weak products, inventory risks, valuable customers, sales risks, channel performance and team bottlenecks.
- Approved AI/automation can execute work instead of only generating recommendations.
- Every important action is permissioned, auditable and measurable.
- The platform owner can package capabilities as plans, services and add-ons without turning SatışDesk into a one-off custom project.

## 2. Business Digital Twin / Revenue Graph

SatışDesk maintains a live operational model of each tenant's commercial business. This is the foundation for intelligence and autonomy.

The model connects:
- Goals and milestones.
- Companies, branches, teams and employees.
- Customers and identities.
- Conversations and activities.
- Leads/opportunities/deals.
- Quotes, approvals and orders.
- Products/services, SKUs and categories.
- Suppliers, inventory, warehouses and stock movements.
- Prices, discounts, costs, revenue and gross margin.
- Channels, campaigns, sources and stores.
- Tasks, automations, recommendations and outcomes.

The system must preserve attribution so it can answer not only **what happened**, but **why it happened and what produced the result**.

Desired trace:

**Campaign/Channel -> Customer -> Conversation -> Employee/Agent -> Quote -> Order -> Product -> Revenue -> Margin -> Goal Contribution**

## 4. Goal Intelligence & Growth Engine — Primary Differentiator

A company can define measurable goals, for example:
- Reach TRY 1,000,000 monthly revenue.
- Sell 5,000 units this quarter.
- Reach 30% gross margin.
- Acquire 1,000 new customers.
- Raise conversion to 20%.
- Reduce overdue follow-ups below 5%.
- Grow a selected product/category/channel.

### 4.1 Goal Model

Each goal requires:
- Metric and target value.
- Baseline value.
- Start/end dates.
- Scope: company, branch, team, employee, product/category or channel.
- Owner/accountable person.
- Milestones.
- Leading and lagging indicators.

### 4.2 Progress & Forecast

Continuously calculate:
- Current vs target.
- Completion percentage.
- Expected progress by current date.
- Actual vs expected trajectory.
- Required daily/weekly/monthly run rate.
- Current run rate.
- Pipeline coverage.
- Forecasted end value.
- Goal-at-risk signal/confidence where evidence supports it.
- Remaining gap.

Example: target TRY 1M/month, current TRY 420K. Do not stop at 42%. Calculate elapsed/remaining selling time, required run rate, current run rate, pipeline coverage and expected result at deadline.

### 4.3 Goal Decomposition

Decompose a target into operational drivers:

**Revenue Target -> Orders Needed -> Qualified Opportunities Needed -> Leads Needed -> Activities/Follow-ups Needed**

And by:
- employee/team,
- product/category,
- branch,
- channel/campaign,
- period.

The owner must see where the target gap originates.

### 4.4 Winning Product Intelligence

For each product/service, where data permits, calculate:
- Units sold.
- Revenue.
- Gross profit and margin.
- Conversion.
- Sales velocity.
- Inventory turnover.
- Return/cancellation rate.
- Discount dependency.
- Channel performance.
- Employee performance selling it.
- Trend vs previous periods.

Actionable classes:
- Winner: strong demand + healthy margin + sustainable stock.
- Growth opportunity: positive trend but underexposed.
- Volume seller / low margin.
- High margin / low volume.
- At risk.
- Dead/slow stock.

Never call a product a winner based on revenue alone.

### 4.5 Goal-to-Action Planning

Explain why a goal is on/off track and propose measurable interventions such as:
- Focus on a high-margin/high-conversion product.
- Recover neglected high-value opportunities.
- Follow up unanswered quotes.
- Increase/reorder stock before a winner stocks out.
- Reduce unnecessary discounting where demand supports it.
- Shift effort from weak channels to stronger channels.
- Coach/assist employees with low conversion or slow response.
- Reactivate valuable previous customers.

Recommendations must be evidence-backed and linked to underlying records.

### 4.6 Closed-Loop Growth

Every material recommendation/action should have:
- Hypothesis/reason.
- Expected outcome when estimable.
- Responsible actor/agent.
- Approval/policy state.
- Execution state.
- Actual result.
- Comparison to baseline/expected result.
- Learning retained for future recommendations where safe.

This creates a defensible learning loop instead of a dashboard.

## 5. Decision & Action Engine

This layer converts intelligence into governed execution.

### 5.1 Next Best Action
Prioritize work based on value, urgency, probability, customer context, goal contribution, SLA and risk.

### 5.2 Policy & Approval Engine

AI does not gain unlimited authority.

Pattern:

**AI proposes -> Policy checks -> Human approves when required -> Tool executes -> Verifier checks -> Audit records**

Examples requiring configurable controls:
- Discounts above threshold.
- Price changes.
- Bulk customer outreach.
- Stock/purchase recommendations or orders.
- Customer-facing AI actions.
- High-impact automation.
- Data export/destructive actions.

### 5.3 Action Outcome Ledger

Record what SatışDesk recommended/executed and the resulting business outcome. This enables ROI measurement and prevents unverifiable AI claims.

## 6. Product, Inventory & Commerce Engine

Required capabilities:
- Products/services, categories, SKU/barcode.
- Cost, selling price, tax and price lists.
- Suppliers.
- Purchase/inbound stock.
- Warehouses/locations.
- Stock movements and reservations.
- Low-stock/reorder alerts.
- Orders and order items.
- Returns/cancellations.
- Cost of goods, revenue, gross profit and margin.

Lifecycle:

**Supplier/Purchase -> Stock In -> Available Inventory -> Quote/Order -> Sale -> Stock Out -> Revenue/Margin -> Return/Adjustment**

Do not become a generic ERP. Commerce depth exists to improve revenue execution, profitability and goal intelligence.

## 7. Quotes, Pricing & Approvals

Lifecycle:

**Customer -> Products/Services -> Quantity -> Price List -> Discount -> Tax -> Quote -> Approval -> Sent -> Viewed -> Accepted/Rejected -> Order/Deal**

Required:
- Quote templates and PDF/print.
- Versioning/status history.
- Discount limits by role.
- Approval workflow above thresholds.
- Price lists/customer-specific pricing when enabled.
- Quote follow-up automation.
- Quote/order attribution to goal, employee, product and channel.

## 10. Analytics, Causal Evidence & BI

Not static metric cards.

Core dimensions:
- Time.
- Company/branch/team/employee.
- Product/category.
- Channel/campaign/source.
- Customer segment.

Core analytics:
- Revenue and margin.
- Funnel/conversion.
- Pipeline and forecast.
- Stage aging.
- Sales velocity.
- Win/loss reasons.
- Employee performance.
- Product performance.
- Inventory turnover.
- Channel ROI/attributed revenue where cost data exists.
- Repeat customers/cohorts where useful.

Every headline metric should be drillable to records that created it.

The system must distinguish correlation from proven causation. When measuring interventions, use before/after, holdouts/experiments or other defensible evidence where feasible. Never claim an AI action caused growth merely because growth followed it.

## 11. AI Revenue & Growth Copilot

AI is an operating intelligence layer, not a decorative chatbot.

Capabilities:
- Conversation summaries.
- Multilingual qualification/routing.
- Lead/customer priority support.
- Suggested replies.
- Next Best Action.
- Deal risk detection.
- Goal gap diagnosis.
- Winning-product/channel insights.
- Quote/follow-up assistance.
- Management briefings.
- Anomaly/opportunity detection.
- Scenario analysis: what changes if conversion, price, stock, channel mix or staffing changes?

AI recommendations must respect tenant isolation, permissions, policy, approvals and auditability.

## 12. Scenario Planning & Revenue Simulation

A global-grade system should help owners evaluate decisions before executing them.

Examples:
- If conversion rises from 12% to 15%, what happens to the monthly target?
- If Product A stocks out in 8 days, what revenue/margin is at risk?
- What happens if we reduce discounting by 3%?
- Which combination of product/channel/team changes has the strongest plausible path to the goal?

Simulation outputs are decision support, clearly separated from actual results and confidence-bounded where appropriate.

## 13. Automation & Agentic Execution

Examples:
- New lead -> qualify/route/assign.
- No response within SLA -> remind/escalate.
- Hot opportunity -> manager alert.
- Overdue follow-up -> escalation.
- Quote unanswered -> follow-up sequence.
- Stalled deal -> rescue workflow.
- Low stock on winning product -> alert/reorder recommendation.
- Goal off-track -> diagnosis and action plan.
- Won order -> next operational workflow.

Long-term execution model supports specialized governed agents rather than one uncontrolled agent. Agents can operate in bounded roles for sales, growth, customer operations, analytics and other approved domains.

Agents share the tenant's governed business context but receive only the tools/data their role permits.

## 14. Independent Verification & Evals

A professional autonomous system needs a verifier, not blind trust in its own AI.

For high-impact intelligence/actions:
- Validate required data exists.
- Check policy/permissions.
- Validate tool result.
- Detect contradictions/anomalies.
- Record failure/retry/handoff.
- Measure recommendation quality and business outcome.

Maintain evals for important AI capabilities such as qualification, routing, recommendations, summaries, action selection and multilingual behavior.

Self-improvement may improve models, prompts, ranking and recommendations, but **must not self-expand authority or bypass policy**.

## 15. Proactive Owner Briefing

The owner should not need to hunt through dashboards.

SatışDesk should proactively deliver concise operational briefings such as:
- What changed since yesterday/last week.
- Goal status and forecast.
- Biggest opportunity.
- Biggest risk.
- Winning/declining products.
- Team bottlenecks.
- Channel changes.
- Decisions awaiting approval.
- Actions executed and verified results.

Delivery can progressively use in-app, email, WhatsApp or other approved channels.

## 17. Security & Governance — Project Security Shield

Applies across the platform:
- Deny-by-default authorization.
- Multi-tenant isolation and Supabase RLS.
- RBAC/ABAC where required.
- IDOR/BOLA and privilege-escalation protection.
- Input/API/AI firewall controls.
- Secrets/service-role protection.
- Secure RPC/SECURITY DEFINER patterns.
- Webhook verification/idempotency.
- Dependency/secret/CI security gates.
- Logging, anomaly detection and audit trail.
- Independent verification/regression before production closure.

Security is a product capability, not only an engineering gate.

## 18. Garfix Relationship

SatışDesk is the business/revenue product. Garfix remains a separate governed autonomous operations platform that can power reusable agentic, verification, monitoring and self-healing capabilities across SatışDesk, Sovereign and future products.

Do not tightly couple SatışDesk's commercial identity to Garfix implementation details. Reusable capabilities should be extracted into Garfix/core services when mature enough, then consumed by SatışDesk through governed interfaces.

## 19. Product Principles — Locked

1. **No traditional CRM positioning.** CRM is an internal capability, not the product identity.
2. **No dashboard theater.** A chart without insight/action value is insufficient.
3. Every major module must solve an operating problem and connect to the business graph.
4. Owner surfaces answer: What changed? Why? What happens next? What should we do? What can the system execute? Did it work?
5. Employee surfaces optimize execution, not management reporting.
6. Platform owner controls commercial packaging without code changes where practical.
7. AI recommendations must be grounded in measurable tenant data.
8. No important metric without traceability/drill-down where feasible.
9. No autonomous high-impact action without policy, permission, audit and verification.
10. Self-improvement must never mean self-expanding authority.
11. Do not become a generic ERP; prioritize Goal -> Customer -> Sale -> Product -> Revenue/Margin -> Intelligence -> Action.
12. Build core capabilities first, then package mature capabilities as sellable services/add-ons.
13. Global readiness is architectural, not a translation pass at the end.
14. A feature is not complete until its outcome can be observed/measured where applicable.
15. No Commercial GO merely because login, CRUD and generic dashboards work.

If the answer is only "it stores data" or "it adds another dashboard," it is not sufficient for the SatışDesk north star.

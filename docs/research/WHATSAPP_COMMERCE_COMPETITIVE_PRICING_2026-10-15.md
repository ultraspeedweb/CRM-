# SatışDesk — WhatsApp Commerce & Revenue OS Competitive Benchmark

Date: 2026-10-15. Status: verified public-price research; not approved SatışDesk list pricing.

## Segments
- WhatsApp-native ordering + store: Take App.
- AI-first omnichannel conversations and CRM: Kommo, respond.io, SleekFlow, WATI.
- Turkish ecommerce infrastructure: ikas, Ticimax.
- SatışDesk's positioning: governed, multilingual commercial Revenue OS. Do not become an isolated storefront or ordinary CRM.

## Product lessons / mapping to existing workstreams
- SD-2 / WS9 Omnichannel: unified customer identity; WhatsApp-first signed and replay-safe messaging; handoff; Instagram later.
- SD-3 / WS10 + Master Plan commerce: catalog, variant, stock, quote, cart/checkout, order, secure payment confirmation, courier, status notifications; do not write financial truth from AI.
- SD-4 / WS11 + Goal engine: attributed margin and profit per product, channel, agent and action; goal forecast and high-value next best action.
- SD-5 / WS12-13: multilingual guided AI sales, approvals, hard guardrails against incorrect prices/stock, measurable assisted conversion.
- SD-6 / WS15: company/subscription pricing, add-ons, Meta/AI usage meters, limits, customer-effective-date contracts.
- SD-7 / WS14: typed and scoped API; external store connectors; no direct DB coupling.

## Value proposition
Do NOT compete on 'WhatsApp store'. Compete on: multilingual (TR/AR/EN) chat-to-profitable-sale execution, manager intelligence, profit-aware recommendations and reliable human/AI workflows. Entry vertical pilot can target Turkish teams selling to multilingual buyers, then widen with measured evidence.

## Proposed pricing hypotheses (NOT approved and NOT published)
- Starter TRY 1,490-2,490/month: 2-3 seats, CRM inbox, follow-ups, core reports. Excludes Meta messaging fees and heavy AI.
- Growth TRY 3,990-6,990/month: 5 seats, approved WhatsApp integration, quotes, automation, assistant quota, enhanced analytics; external channel fees separate.
- Business TRY 9,990-17,990/month: multi-branch, roles, profit + stock analytics, larger quotas, approved AI agents, premium support.
- Enterprise: negotiated with onboarding/integration fee and defined SLA.
These are test ranges for willingness-to-pay and unit economics, not marketing promises. Test 10/100/1,000 tenant gross margins, usage, invoice/VAT, conversion, and customer interviews before fixing public prices. Consider the existing managed paid pilot as a separate services offer rather than mixing it with SaaS subscription.

## Validation gates
1. Complete existing commercial WS7 closure first. No production scope expansion in this research PR.

3. Build feature-scorecard by customer jobs and actual working APIs; separate claimed vs hands-on verified.

5. Conduct at least 8-12 qualified discovery interviews and 2-4 pilot demos; record willingness-to-pay.
6. Product owner approves final prices; staging verifies entitlements/usage and billing before public publication.
